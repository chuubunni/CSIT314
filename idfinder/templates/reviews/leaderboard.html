How is the score worked out?
  • Star rating – a Bayesian average: with few reviews a designer stays close to the platform average, so one 5-star review cannot beat a long track record. Newer reviews count more (a review loses half its weight every 60 days).
  • Review sentiment – how positive the written reviews are, scored by text analysis ({{ engine }}) and adjusted the same way.
  • Customer interest – how often customers shortlist their projects after viewing them, compared with the platform average.
  • Reliability – accepts requests, finishes the jobs they accept, and replies to reviews.
  • Experience – completed jobs, with diminishing returns.

Only visible reviews count. Reviews hidden by the platform manager are ignored, and only customers with a completed job can review.
