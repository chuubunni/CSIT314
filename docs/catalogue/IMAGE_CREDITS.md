# Image credits and dataset notes

These are reference images for fictional coursework projects. They are not evidence of work carried out by the listed designers, or photographs of the listed Singapore locations or housing types.
Each image was visually reviewed for room content, a matching caption and dominant visible style. Styles can overlap; this is a curated coursework catalogue, not a formally annotated machine-learning benchmark.
The replacement pool includes stock and staged interior references, and some concept renders. No new images were generated for this package.
